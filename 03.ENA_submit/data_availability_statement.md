# Data Availability Statement

All raw RNA-seq reads generated in this study have been deposited in the
European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) at EMBL-EBI under project accession
**PRJEB126112** (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJEB126112).
Data are held private until **2026-11-01** and can be released earlier from the
Webin portal when the paper is accepted.

This study is associated with the preprint: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.04.749384v1 (DOI: 10.64898/2026.09.04.749384).
